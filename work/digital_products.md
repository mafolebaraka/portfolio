# Digital products and ebooks

**Role:** Author, researcher, publisher and marketer, 2026
**Audience:** African and Tanzanian freelancers, virtual assistants and translators targeting global clients
**Channels:** My own email list, YouTube, WhatsApp, Nestuge sales pages, Selar and Flutterwave checkout

Selling to my own audience is how I test whether the content is actually useful. These are the products I have written, packaged and marketed in 2026.

## The VA Toolbox

A free ebook and spreadsheet listing 154 tools used by virtual assistants across 18 categories. Every entry covers what the tool is, its pricing in USD, one advantage and one disadvantage, with free tiers and low cost options highlighted for African VAs. Published under the @mafolebaraka brand as a Word ebook plus an Excel sheet. A lead magnet for the Remotika Africa list.

## Get Paid To Translate

A paid ebook on landing online translation work, aimed at African freelancers targeting global clients. Backed by original research lists of 504 translation agencies and 500 virtual assistant agencies with freelancer registration pages. Planned as a printed hardcopy as well as a digital edition.

## Translation Agency Database

A Swahili sales page ("Mawakala 500 wanaotoa kazi za kutafsiri") selling a database of 500 agencies that offer Swahili translation jobs, bundled with sample CVs, a private WhatsApp group and a free masterclass on getting translation jobs from agencies. Buyers are automatically added to a live session capped at 100 seats. Marketed to my mailing list and YouTube audience, where the free translation class announcement was the largest single send on the list (8,190 subscribers).

## What the products taught me

Swahili language sends outperform English ones to this audience. A free class is the best top of funnel for a paid database. And bundling a community (a WhatsApp group, a live session) with a static product is what makes people buy.

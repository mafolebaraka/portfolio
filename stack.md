# Stack · The tools behind the work

What I run, what each tool does in my system, and what it has produced. Figures come from the tools' own dashboards and APIs, pulled on 26 September 2026 unless dated otherwise.

## Email marketing

**Flodesk** · The Remotika Africa list. 10,023 active subscribers, built from zero in February 2026 (4,223 in the first month). 25 campaigns and 70,331 sends at 98.3% delivery, 37.0% open, 6.4% click and 0.32% unsubscribe. Five opt in forms with a combined 77.0% conversion (7,244 visitors, 5,577 opt ins); the free translation masterclass form converts at 84.3%. Three automations, including a Swahili welcome sequence at 71.0% open and 57.6% click across 942 entries. Best send: 72.3% open and 49.6% click. Insight from the data: Sunday and Saturday sends open best, two thirds of clicks are on mobile, Swahili subject lines outperform English.

**Plunk** · Where the Remotika list moved in mid 2026 after the WordPress contacts (users, FluentCRM, Fluent Forms) were consolidated. 12,900 sends in July 2026 at a 0.008% complaint rate.

**FluentCRM and Fluent Forms** · Contact management and every intake form on remotikaafrica.org: cohort interest, apprenticeship applications, event registration, vendor recruitment (one form drew 523 entries in a week).

**Mailchimp** · The Rosalynn in Purple newsletter (The Purple Circle and The Purple Letter): 823 send launch at 23.7% open, sends up 78.5% from Q1 to Q2 2026.

**Substack and beehiiv** · Substack for the Remotika Africa publication and the Grey Area's daily notes (a year of notes written in bulk); beehiiv for Digital Dose by Mafolebaraka, a remote jobs newsletter.

**Wix Groups and Members** · The free on site community on mafolebaraka.com (four groups, launched September 2026).

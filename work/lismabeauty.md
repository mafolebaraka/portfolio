# LismaBeauty · Shopify theme for a premium Dar es Salaam salon

**Role:** Web builder and brand translator, 2026
**Client:** LismaBeauty, a high profile hair salon in Dar es Salaam serving celebrities and general clientele
**Stack:** Shopify (Liquid), HTML, CSS with custom properties, vanilla JavaScript with AJAX

## Brief

Translate the salon's premium positioning into a storefront that handles real ecommerce and booking workflows, replacing a v1 theme whose gaps were placeholder products, empty service blocks and missing testimonial and gallery content.

## What shipped in v2

* Dark and light mode toggle with persistence
* Mobile first responsive design with fluid typography and a slide in mobile navigation
* Interactive booking calendar with month navigation, date and time slot selection and a confirmation flow, available both as a homepage section and as a standalone booking page, with a WhatsApp fallback
* AJAX cart drawer with live item removal and quick add product cards
* Full template suite: product, collection, cart, page, contact, search, blog, article, 404 and customer account pages
* DM Sans as the body typeface

## What I learned

Scope on client projects expands progressively (landing page copy became a full theme, then a v2), so build for extensibility from the first commit. Auditing the live store mid project separates real gaps from assumed ones. And in East Africa a WhatsApp fallback for booking is not a nice to have; it is the channel most clients will actually use.

# Timeline

A year by year view of the work in this portfolio.

## 2021

* Launch @mafolebaraka, a Swahili first channel on online work and freelancing, while studying Communication Studies at the University of Dar es Salaam
* Panel speaker at Dar Slush 2021 and Tanzania Innovation Week 2021

## 2022

* Panel speaker at Sahara Spark 2022
* Write the influencer marketing brief used for Grey Finance's Tanzania market entry

## 2023

* January: join Grey Finance (YC W22) as East Africa Community Manager, the founding community role for the region
* Run community challenges including #28DaysWithGrey; over the Grey years, support product launches in markets including India and Brazil

## 2024

* Move to Content Marketer at Grey: educational content pipeline, Medium essays, a year of Substack notes
* Found Remotika Africa, a freelancing and remote work education movement for young East Africans, and build its 12 week cohort curriculum
* December: launch the mafolebaraka YouTube channel

## 2025

* Become Global Community Manager of The Grey Area, running the community across 70+ countries
* Design Greybot, an AI community agent blueprint on Circle
* Begin content strategy for Rosalynn in Purple through Jukwaa Africa Limited and PZG Impact Firm
* December: complete the BA in Communication Studies, University of Dar es Salaam

## 2026

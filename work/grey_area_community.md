# Grey Finance (YC W22) · Four years, four roles

**Roles:** #Greypartner influencer (2022), East Africa Community Manager, founding role (2023 to 2024), Content Marketer (2024 to 2025), Global Community Manager of The Grey Area (2025 to 2026)
**Platforms:** Telegram (East Africa community), Circle (The Grey Area at community.grey.co), X, Instagram, WhatsApp, Medium, Substack
**Audience:** Freelancers, remote workers and digital nomads; East Africa first, then 70+ countries

## Context

Grey is a cross border fintech backed by Y Combinator (W22) that gives freelancers and remote workers foreign currency accounts, virtual cards and currency exchange. I worked with Grey in four successive roles from its Tanzania launch in September 2022 until August 2026. Over those years the company grew from a few hundred thousand users to more than three million, and I was on the team through product launches in markets including India and Brazil.

## Phase 1 · Tanzania launch influencer campaign (September to October 2022)

Onboarded as a creator on Grey's Tanzania influencer campaign with weekly content across Instagram, X and WhatsApp under #GoGlobalWithGrey. I wrote the Swahili launch threads, proposed and scripted the run of show for the "Kazi za Kidijitali" X Space that Grey hosted from its own account, and organised a Grey sponsored freelancers meetup in Dar es Salaam for around 50 people. Grey's content lead wrote that the content "look really good and it's clear you put a lot of work and effort into it", and closed the campaign with "This campaign has been very successful, and you're one of the reasons". Grey interviewed me for a community role six weeks later.

## Phase 2 · East Africa Community Manager (January 2023 to early 2024)

The founding community role for the region. I wrote the East Africa community strategy (objectives, personas, a target of 1,500 members and three AMAs by the end of March 2023) and ran the community on Telegram with monthly reports to leadership.

* April 2023: community at 1,203 members, up from 829, with 374 joining in a single month; a knowledge sharing session on content monetisation (25 attendees) and an AMA on personal branding (247 registrations)
* June to July 2023: 1,500 to 1,600 members, 30,600 messages sent, 44 merchandise items dispatched to members, a badge system, giveaways and member testimonials
* Monthly content calendars for Grey East Africa and Grey Kenya (foreign accounts, transfers, virtual cards, Grey Tags, tuition payments, Upwork payouts)
* Represented Grey at the Westerwelle Startup Haus Arusha DigiTalent freelancing payments workshop (June 2023) and at Meet Grey events

## Phase 3 · Content Marketer (2024 to 2025)

Wrote the educational content pipeline for Grey's communities: freelancing and solopreneur lessons for the Share and Learn space, Medium essays adapted for a broader audience, a year of Substack notes for daily posting, and two 50 article content libraries (product knowledge and Money Monday) for The Grey Area spaces. Coordinated guest speakers for Grey AMAs such as "Navigating freelance marketplaces" (March 2024) and "How to Leverage Social Media for Professional Branding" (February 2024), and recorded sessions for the UpGreyed creator series.

## Phase 4 · Global Community Manager, The Grey Area (2025 to August 2026)

The Grey Area launched on Circle in January 2024 as Grey's official community for freelancers, remote workers and digital nomads. I ran it globally.

**Scale (as of June 2026):** 13,565 registered accounts, 5,300+ community members across 70+ countries, 10+ spaces, 21+ live events, and a single AMA that drew 4,900+ RSVPs. Knowledge sharing sessions typically drew 26 to 46 RSVPs. The UpGreyed Her grant programme for women creators ran inside the community, with a 2026 "Still winning" live session for the 2025 runners up.

**Programming.** Monthly engagement challenges (#28DaysWithGrey in February 2026, #31DaysOfGettingaNewClient, #31DaysOfBeingFinanciallyIndependent, #TheGreyAreaAugChallenge) built as themed weeks with daily prompts mixed by format: polls, emoji only replies, tag a member days, GIF and photo days, and text prompts reserved for reflection late in the cycle. Weekly "stars" posts, member of the month, a Wall of Love campaign, professional badges, a 2026 events survey, Moments by Mainstack ticket giveaways, and the launch of member to member messaging in June 2026.

**Member Connections.** Launched the Member Connections programme, cited internally as strong work alongside the community data analysis.

**Strategy and analysis.** An international growth strategy targeting 5,000 new members outside Nigeria across India, LATAM, MENA and East Africa, grounded in real Circle data and iterated across three versions. A Q3 2026 plan built on a North Star of Monthly Engaged Members, driven by Reddit and Quora replies, community board listings, guest posts, an in community referral loop and social group distribution, with Kenya, India, the Philippines and the US and UK diaspora as strategic markets. The H1 2026 Community Audit comparing H2 2025 against H1 2026 across audience, content, engagement and stickiness, which reframed the internal conversation from acquisition to activation. A three stage member milestone automation plan for retention.

**AI community agent.** Designed Greybot, a full blueprint for an AI agent on Circle's native AI feature: knowledge base, use cases, system prompts, escalation logic to human support for sensitive issues and a QA framework, written up as a leadership proposal.

**Launch support.** Community and social support for the August 2026 launch of Pouch (savings), Lifestyle (eSIM, airtime, data, bills and rewards) and card points under "More ways to use your Grey balance", including a challenge themed entirely around saving in the month before launch.

## What I learned

Lower barrier formats (reactions, votes, GIFs) drive breadth of participation; text prompts earn depth only once people are warmed up. A regional community on Telegram and a global one on Circle need different rhythms: the first runs on chat velocity, the second on programming. Activation matters more than acquisition once a community passes a few thousand members. And the posting cadence has to be non negotiable.

# Training, speaking and media

**Role:** Trainer, facilitator, panellist, speaker and interviewee, 2021 to 2026
**Subject:** Freelancing, remote work, the gig economy, digital skills, content creation and the creator economy
**Languages:** Swahili and English

Six years of taking the channel offstage. Grouped by type, most recent first within each group.

## Keynotes and TEDx

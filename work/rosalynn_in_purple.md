# Rosalynn in Purple · Content strategy for an executive coaching brand

**Role:** Digital and Social Media Strategist, Community Manager and Analyst, January to September 2026 (eight months), through PZG Impact Firm and Jukwaa Africa Limited
**Client:** Rosalynn Mndolwa-Mworia, Managing Director of Mwananchi Communications Limited and The Citizen
**Channels:** Five social platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, X), a WhatsApp channel, The Purple Circle and The Purple Letter newsletters, YouTube, opinion pieces in The Citizen, rosalynn.co.tz

## Context

Rosalynn in Purple is a personal brand built around women's leadership, ambition and personal growth, particularly for African women. I consulted through the brand's pilot quarter and then ran content strategy, community management, analytics and campaign production, working with the PZG team (video editing, design, WhatsApp channel management) and reporting to PZG's Chief People Officer, who wrote at the end of the pilot: "Thank you so much for your wisdom as we pilot and for consulting with us throughout Q2."

## What I did

**Editorial programme.** Seven monthly themed campaigns with a guardrail that all copy must empower the audience: Q1 "Leading from Your Why: Purpose as Your Strategic North Star", April "Future of Truth" and "AI and Leadership", May "Integration Over Balance". Deliverables included opinion pieces for The Citizen, LinkedIn articles, trending format Instagram captions, engagement polls and weekly open ended questions, with poll content kept distinct across Instagram, WhatsApp and newsletter formats.

**Newsletter.** Launched The Purple Letter (issue 4, "AI and Digital Leadership", April 2026) and managed The Purple Circle subscriber re engagement with segmented re introduction emails for Rising Woman event attendees and one to one contacts, plus a Mailchimp versus Flodesk platform comparison.

**Website relaunch (RIPP 2.0).** A 30 day pre launch, launch week and post launch social plan for the new rosalynn.co.tz (April 2026), a 13 page HTML site build, and a five day pre launch campaign for the first version of the site.

**Audit and SEO.** A May 2026 audit of the site's nine pages against 13 reference resources, with the headline finding that the brand had essentially no third party backlinks despite 30+ speaking engagements and a regular column in The Citizen. I mapped 47 media placements across 22 outlets, catalogued seven Citizen op eds, and identified 21 priority link opportunities, then wrote a revised link building plan.

**The Purple Talk video show.** Designed the format and wrote two scripted episodes. Launch content (social captions, guest tease posts, a father reveal post, SVG artwork) and full episode briefs in a two part format: a Creative Directory (note to the team, creative guidance for the host, pre record checklist, B roll shot list, cold open) and a Run of Show (numbered segments, verbatim scripted questions, a "why this" rationale and clip targets per segment). Flagship episodes target 35 to 50 minutes and cut into 6 to 10 vertical clips. Episodes developed: "Raising Human Beings: What Children Learn When We Are Not Looking" with educator Dr Edmund Mndolwa, and "Will AI Close the Leadership Gap for African Women, or Widen It?" with Irene Phoebe Kiwia of Adanian Labs and AICE Africa.

**Reporting.** A granular January to March technical performance report for internal use, a client facing version, monthly GA4 and Search Console reviews, and a close out report in September 2026 covering objectives, results, challenges and recommendations.

**Brand voice.** Every piece written in Rosalynn's verified voice: British spelling, no em dashes, a "Rosalynn, in Purple" sign off, and the hashtag set #RosalynnInPurple #WomenWhoLead #AfricanLeadership.

## Results (from the September 2026 close out report)

* Newsletter launch: 823 sends at a 23.7% open rate (above the roughly 21% category benchmark) and 2.1% click; newsletter sends up 78.5% from Q1 to Q2 (1,341 to 2,394)
* Website engagement time up more than 300% in one month (52 seconds to 3 minutes 37 seconds); Q2 average session 4 minutes 56 seconds, 4.1 times the starting point
* Organic search sessions up 3.2 times (30 to 97); direct traffic up 54.5%; the site recorded its first Search Console click milestones in April 2026
* TikTok profile visits up 12 times; Facebook profile visits up 83.7%; LinkedIn reach up 37.2%
* Seven monthly themed campaigns delivered, the RIPP 2.0 relaunch completed, and a show format and brief template now used for every episode of The Purple Talk
* A measurement baseline with UTM tracking and downloadable lead magnets identified as the next gaps to close; testimonial content sourced only from real follower comments and DMs

PZG closed the engagement on 3 September 2026 with thanks for my "contributions and creativity".

## What I learned

Surface problems alongside solutions: my reports were transparent about limitations, paired with concrete recommendations and framed as opportunities. An executive with a newspaper column and 30 speaking engagements a year can still have zero backlinks; earned media only counts when someone links it. Get a trend's mechanics right before writing to it. And never fabricate social proof; source it.

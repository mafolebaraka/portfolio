# Remotika Space · Partner discount portal

**Role:** Founder, product owner and builder, 2026
**Site:** [space.remotikaafrica.org](https://space.remotikaafrica.org)
**Stack:** WordPress on own hosting, mobile money and card payments in TZS, QR code digital membership cards

## The idea

Freelancers in Dar es Salaam pay for coworking desks, meeting rooms, courses, hotels and restaurants out of irregular income. Remotika Space turns the Remotika Africa audience into a buying group: members pay a small annual fee, partners give them a discount, and the community gets a reason to keep its membership active.

## Model

* Three annual tiers: TZS 5,000 for 5% off, TZS 10,000 for 15% off, TZS 20,000 for 20% off
* Discounts cover coworking spaces, course providers, meeting rooms, hotels, restaurants and other service providers
* Service providers register themselves free, list their services and wait for approval before going live
* Members are verified at the counter with a digital membership card carrying a QR code

## Why it matters as marketing

It gives partners a low cost way to reach a defined audience of young remote workers, gives members a tangible reason to pay, and gives Remotika Africa a recurring revenue line that does not depend on course launches.

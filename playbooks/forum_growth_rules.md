# Forum and community led growth rules

The rules I set for The Grey Area's Q3 2026 growth strategy, where the North Star was Monthly Engaged Members and growth came from forum replies, community board listings, articles and guest posts, an in community referral loop and social group distribution.

## Reddit and Quora replies

* Never mention that you work at the company in a forum reply. You are a member of the community answering a question.
* Always hyperlink the products you mention, including competitors. A reply that only links your own product reads as spam and gets removed.
* Never ask people to DM you. Answer in public.
* Recommend your product, your business product or your community only where each honestly fits the corridor or use case in the question. If a competitor is the better fit, say so.
* Embed subreddits naturally in the body of a reply and close with a "Where to dig further" block of relevant subreddit links.

## Community board listings

List the community on every directory and board where the target audience looks for communities, with a consistent description and a tracked link.

## Articles and guest posts

Adapt community lessons into essays for Medium and guest posts on partner sites. Strip community language for the broader audience. Each essay links back to one specific space, not the homepage.

## In community referral loop

Give existing members a reason and a mechanism to invite one person: a challenge day built around tagging a friend, a referral link in the welcome sequence, and recognition for members who bring people in.

## Social group distribution

Share community threads into WhatsApp, Telegram and Facebook groups where the target market already gathers, with a member's voice, not the brand's.

## Market focus

Pick three or four strategic markets and write the plan per market. For The Grey Area those were Kenya, India, the Philippines and the US and UK diaspora. A global plan with no market focus is not a plan.

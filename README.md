# Baraka Mafole · Marketing Portfolio

Content marketer, community builder and bilingual creator (English and Swahili) based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. I plan content, produce it, distribute it, and report on what it did. This repository holds case studies of my work from 2021 to 2026, the numbers behind them, the playbooks I reuse across clients, and the products I have shipped.

Profile: [github.com/mafolebaraka](https://github.com/mafolebaraka) · Website: [mafolebaraka.com](https://mafolebaraka.com) · Everywhere: @mafolebaraka

## How to read this

Each case study follows the same shape: context, my role, what I did, results, and what I learned. Numbers are real, dated and sourced; the [results dashboard](results.md) collects all of them in one place, the [stack](stack.md) page shows which tools produced them, and the [timeline](timeline.md) puts the work in order. If you only read two files, read [how I built my personal brand](work/personal_brand.md), because the audience it produced is what every other project here was built on, and [Grey Finance](work/grey_area_community.md), four years and four roles inside one Y Combinator fintech.

The thread through all of it: content builds the audience, email and community keep it, and the numbers prove it. I have done that for a Y Combinator fintech, for executives and brands, and for my own ventures, in Swahili and English.

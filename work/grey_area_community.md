# The Grey Area · Grey Finance (YC W22)

**Role:** East Africa Community Manager (founding role, 2023 to 2024), Content Marketer (2024 to 2025), Global Community Manager (2025 to 2026)
**Platform:** Circle (community.grey.co)
**Audience:** Freelancers, remote workers and digital nomads across 70+ countries

## Context

Grey is a cross border fintech backed by Y Combinator (W22) that gives freelancers and remote workers foreign currency accounts, virtual cards and currency exchange. The Grey Area is its official community. I joined in January 2023 as the first community hire for East Africa and grew into running the community globally.

Earlier, I wrote the influencer marketing brief used for Grey's Tanzania market entry in 2022, and I was part of the team that supported product launches in markets including India and Brazil.

## What I did

**Community operations.** Member welcome replies, weekly "stars" posts recognising top contributors, member of the month selections, merchandise conversations with members, a Wall of Love campaign, and daily moderation across spaces. Member DMs were written to read like a real person typing, not a branded template.

**Monthly challenge programming.** Designed and ran structured engagement challenges including #28DaysWithGrey, #31DaysOfBeingFinanciallyIndependent, #31DaysOfGettingaNewClient and #TheGreyAreaAugChallenge. Each ran as themed weeks with daily prompts calibrated by format: polls, emoji only replies, tag a member days, GIF and photo days, and text prompts reserved for reflection moments late in the cycle. Communications were segmented by member history (previous participants versus new members).

**Educational content pipeline.** Turned freelancing and solopreneur source material into community ready lessons for the Share and Learn space, then adapted the same material into Medium essays and a year of Substack notes for distribution outside the community.

**Growth strategy.** Wrote an international growth strategy targeting 5,000 new members outside Nigeria across India, LATAM, MENA and East Africa, grounded in real Circle data and iterated across three versions. For Q3 2026 I set a North Star of Monthly Engaged Members with growth driven by forum replies on Reddit and Quora, community board listings, articles and guest posts, an in community referral loop, and social group distribution. Strategic markets were Kenya, India, the Philippines and the US and UK diaspora.

**Community audit.** Ran the H1 2026 Community Audit comparing H2 2025 against H1 2026 across audience, content, engagement and stickiness metrics, and reframed the internal conversation from acquisition to activation.

**AI community agent.** Designed Greybot, a full blueprint for an AI agent on Circle's native AI feature: knowledge base, use cases, system prompts, escalation logic to human support for sensitive issues, and a QA framework. Written up as a leadership proposal.

**Product launch support.** Community and social support for the H2 2026 launch of Pouch (savings), Lifestyle (eSIM, airtime, data, bills and rewards) and card points under the umbrella message "More ways to use your Grey balance", including a warm up challenge themed entirely around saving in the month before launch.

**Member Connections.** Launched the Member Connections programme, cited internally as strong work alongside the community data analysis.

## Results

* Community footprint of more than 5,000 members across 70+ countries
* Repeatable monthly challenge format adopted as the community's engagement backbone
* Growth strategy documents adopted as the planning base for international expansion
* Greybot blueprint delivered as the community's AI support design
* Recognised strengths in the role: community data analysis, authentic member communication, and ideation

## What I learned

Lower barrier formats (reactions, votes, GIFs) drive breadth of participation, and text prompts earn depth only once people are warmed up. Activation matters more than acquisition once a community passes a few thousand members. And a community manager's job is half content, half operations: the posting cadence has to be non negotiable.

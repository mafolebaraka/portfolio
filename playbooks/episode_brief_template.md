# Video show episode brief template

The two part brief used for every episode of The Purple Talk, the Rosalynn in Purple video show. Flagship episodes target 35 to 50 minutes and are cut into 6 to 10 vertical clips.

## Header

* Working episode title
* Host
* Guest
* Format
* Target runtime
* Why this guest (two or three sentences)
* Through line (the one idea the whole episode serves)

## Part 1 · Creative Directory

**Note to the team.** What this episode is for, what success looks like, and anything the crew must know.

**Creative guidance to the host.** Tone, energy, what to lean into, what to avoid, moments to slow down.

**Pre record checklist.** Guest confirmed and briefed, questions shared or withheld (state which), set, wardrobe, audio, B roll plan, release forms.

**B roll shot list.** Specific shots to capture before or after the conversation.

**Cold open.** The first 30 to 60 seconds, scripted or described, that earns the viewer's next minute.

## Part 2 · Run of Show

Numbered segments. For each segment:

* **Segment title and target duration**
* **Scripted questions, verbatim.** The host can depart from them, but they exist.
* **Why this.** One or two lines on what the segment is meant to surface.
* **Clip targets.** The vertical clips expected from this segment, with a working hook for each.

## Rules

* Every episode must produce its clip targets. If a segment has no clip target, cut it or merge it.
* Questions are written in the host's verified voice.
* The through line appears in the cold open and in the closing segment.

# Miss Universe Tanzania 2026 · Influencer campaign

**Role:** Campaign strategist and influencer manager, 2026
**Platforms:** TikTok and Instagram, video only
**Runway:** Four to six weeks to the finale

## Brief

Build awareness for the whole competition, introduce individual contestants to the public, and reach audiences outside Tanzania, using creators who already own their communities. The structural reference was the influencer marketing brief I wrote for Grey's Tanzania market entry in 2022.

## Campaign structure

**Amplifiers (whole competition).** Three Instagram creators posting daily for a month on content supplied by the campaign: Maimartha (@maimarthajesse), Juma Lokole (@jumalokole_02) and Mwanachuo Family. Budget TZS 1M, 1M and 400k respectively.

**International amplifier.** Iam Ndeshi, daily for a month, given content access to do her own analysis. Budget TZS 1M.

**Niche analysts (individual contestants).** Afro Swagga (@afroswagga) on Instagram and Judith Ene Laizer (@judith.ene.laizer) on TikTok, each posting their own contestant analysis at least three times a week. A nano tier of at least ten creators to endorse and introduce individual contestants.

**Lifestyle crossover.** Three creators at TZS 1M each for three videos plus one static post, chosen because food, money and fitness are three niches women are deeply invested in, so contestants appear inside content people already watch:
* Anisa Kitenge (food and culture) takes a contestant to a local market for miguu ya kuku and wali maharage, then cooks with her using clean energy
* Neema Chamy (@neema_chamy, money and finance) asks a contestant money questions
* Fit Freya TZ (@fitfreyatz, fitness) asks contestants their favourite exercise and trains with them

## Process

Outreach opened with a request for each creator's rate card before the brief was shared. The roster was pruned as rate cards came in. A recommended total budget was prepared for the client since no fixed budget existed at the start. Some crossover sessions were designed so they could become TV show content.

## Partner work

A 60 second TVC concept for the Kidoti Foundation as a Miss Universe Tanzania partner, working title "Fursa kwa Wasusi", built around hairdressers and the theme of inclusivity and diversity.

## What the campaign shows

Tiering by job (amplify, reach abroad, analyse, cross over) is clearer than tiering by follower count alone. And creators with their own communities give the campaign distribution the client cannot buy with ads.

# Influencer campaign tiering

A four tier roster model for consumer campaigns in East Africa. Used for the Miss Universe Tanzania 2026 campaign and descended from the influencer marketing brief I wrote for Grey's Tanzania market entry in 2022.

## Tier by job, not by follower count

**1. Amplifiers.** Two or three large creators whose job is to promote the whole campaign. They post daily for the campaign window on content the campaign supplies. Highest fee, least creative freedom.

**2. International reach.** One creator based abroad or with a diaspora audience, given content access and freedom to do their own analysis. Their job is awareness outside the home market.

**3. Niche analysts.** Creators inside the campaign's subject niche who post their own take at least three times a week. Their job is credibility and depth. Below them, a nano tier of ten or more small creators to endorse and introduce individual elements (contestants, products, features).

**4. Lifestyle crossover.** Creators in adjacent niches the target audience already lives in. For a women's audience in Tanzania that is food, money and fitness. Their job is to put the campaign inside content people already watch, with a fixed package (for example three videos plus one static post) and a specified video concept per creator.

## Process

1. Outreach opens with a request for the creator's rate card. Do not share the brief until the rate card is in.
2. Build the roster and prune it as rate cards arrive. Drop creators whose rates do not match their role.
3. Where the client has no fixed budget, prepare a recommended budget by tier and take it forward for approval.
4. Specify the deliverable per creator in one line: platform, format, frequency, window, and the concept for crossover creators.
5. Design at least some sessions so they can be repurposed as broadcast or TV content.

## Why this works here

Creators with their own communities give the campaign distribution the client cannot buy with ads. Crossover content injects the campaign naturally rather than interrupting the feed. And tiering by job makes the budget conversation about outcomes instead of follower counts.

# The early years · 2021 to 2022

**Role:** Freelancer, Swahili digital skills educator, community organiser, agency social media manager, startup founder
**Platforms:** X (Twitter Spaces), YouTube, Telegram, Zoom, Clubhouse

Everything in this portfolio started here, while I was a Communication Studies undergraduate at the University of Dar es Salaam. This is the period where I learned to build an audience with nothing but a phone and a Twitter account.

## Selling the first classes on Twitter (2021)

In May 2021 I launched a paid freelancing class from my Twitter account. Bookings arrived by email in Swahili and English ("Darasa la freelancing", "Nataka kujifunza freelancing"): a four day one to one course on Zoom where the learner set the schedule, with a first batch of 20 seats, and group classes by July. Alongside it I organised "Digital Opportunities for Youth", my first free online meetup (16 May 2021, with Media Convergency), and a recurring evening series, Freelancing saa tatu (July to August 2021). Between gigs I freelanced myself on Upwork, Fiverr, Guru and PeoplePerHour, mostly Swahili translation, voice over and localisation work, which is why the advice was always first hand.

## Sanuka Kidigitali (2021 to 2022)

Co founded with Leyla Mohamed, Sanuka Kidigitali was an initiative helping young Tanzanians acquire digital skills through YouTube, Telegram and Twitter. We ran workshop programmes with university and village kickoffs, an Arusha Digital Skills Masterclass (June 2022), and the #DundaKidijitali Twitter Spaces series with guests from the local tech scene (February to May 2022). Sahara Ventures invited us to co develop a Dar es Salaam Digital Skills Lab, and the Tanzania Startup Association engaged us as research consultants on its study of the digital freelancing sector.

## Social Media Day Tanzania (30 June 2022)

As coordinator, I ran the partnership drive for Social Media Day in Tanzania, sending a concept note and budget to more than 20 organisations (TSA, Media Convergency, Bolt, Yellow Card, Serengeti Bytes, Global Peace Foundation, DOT Trust among them), securing PZG Impact as sponsor of branded materials, the UDSM Centre for Communication Studies as venue partner, and a speaker line up. Remotika Africa revived the day in 2025.

## Agency and client social media work (2021 to 2022)

* **Shugulika Africa** (via SIA Square agency, May to June 2021): account audit and recommendations report covering before and after engagement rate and content quality. My first audit, and the ancestor of the [audit framework](../playbooks/social_media_audit.md).
* **Outreach Bin** (June 2021): social media management strategy with goals, KPIs (audience growth, mentions, impressions, conversions), content mix and tools.
* **Unleashed Africa Social Ventures, RISEUP learning lab** (April 2021): sourced and uploaded curated content across health, skills, innovation, sports and news within a gender context.
* **Tanzania World Marathon** (Sonetra Sports Agency, November 2021 to February 2022): created and managed the event's Twitter account for three months.
* **Prudence Zoe Glorious (PZG)** (from May 2022): Twitter growth strategy with a target of 166 new followers a month, content pillars (#digitalspacewithprudence, #uchumijukwaa, conscious living, purpose leads), a weekly schedule and an insight sheet; monthly content calendars; and the DigitalSpaceWithPrudence content collaboration that started in 2021. This became a four year relationship that led to the Rosalynn in Purple engagement.
* **Myboldafro** (August 2021): motivational quote design templates.
* Lead generation on contract for CANEI Corp via Upwork (2021).

## Jukwaa Africa (founded August 2022)

Founded Jukwaa Africa as a startup "focused to shape the future of the creator economy", building a digital products marketplace and a creator mini page platform. As COO I wrote the concept note, strategic plan and pitch deck, contracted Swahilies for API documentation and a UX and UI design partner for the system, ran investor calls with Rarebreed VC, Benue Capital and Kacha.io (April 2023), joined the Tanzania Startup Association, and proposed a Jukwaa Academy freelancing training programme (March 2023). The marketplace shipped in 2026 as [jukwaa.co](https://jukwaa.co); see [platforms.md](platforms.md).

## What this period taught me

Audience first, product second: the classes sold because the tweets had already taught people something for free. Partnerships are a numbers game; twenty concept notes produced one sponsor and one venue. And the best content for East African freelancers comes from people who have actually been paid by a client abroad.

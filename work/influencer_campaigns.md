# Brand campaigns as a creator

**Role:** Influencer, content creator and campaign reporter, 2022 to 2026
**Platforms:** X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, WhatsApp
**Audience:** Young Tanzanians and East Africans interested in digital skills, careers and money

Before I managed influencer campaigns for clients, I was the influencer. These are the brand campaigns I delivered as a creator, with the numbers the clients and I reported.

## Education in Ireland awareness campaign (Government of Ireland agency, via PZG Impact), February and October 2022

One of four Tanzanian influencers. Two Swahili "Study in Ireland" videos plus tweets in the first wave, and scripts and tweets for a second #EduInIreland wave in October. Google Analytics for the campaign attributed **458 users (77.6% of all influencer driven traffic)**, 451 new users and 532 sessions with an average session of 3 minutes 32 seconds to my content, and **120 of the campaign's 147 completed registrations (81.5%)**. PZG's performance review congratulated me for "making the Education in Ireland Awareness Campaign a success".

## Grey Finance Tanzania launch, #Greypartner (September to October 2022)

Weekly content across Instagram, X and WhatsApp for Grey's Tanzania market entry, the Swahili #GoGlobalWithGrey threads, a scripted X Space hosted from Grey's account, and a Grey sponsored freelancers meetup for around 50 people in Dar es Salaam. Grey called the campaign "very successful" and named me as one of the reasons, then hired me as its founding East Africa Community Manager. The full story is in [grey_area_community.md](grey_area_community.md).

## Africa's Business Heroes (Jack Ma Foundation, via Arora Online), September to December 2023

Profiled the 2022 Tanzanian finalist Elia Timotheo in an Instagram video and story, then travelled to Kigali as a sponsored guest and content creator for the ABH Summit and Grand Finale (23 to 24 November 2023) with access to interview the Top 10. The promotion generated **500,000+ video views**. Arora thanked me for "unwavering support for ABH 2023 and for your absolute professionalism", and I turned the trip into a Swahili podcast episode on what I learned about content creation in Rwanda.

## Power Learn Project (Africa), influencer campaign, April to May 2024

A three week campaign across LinkedIn, X and Instagram to recruit students to Power Learn Project's programmes in Tanzania, reported on 9 May 2024: **449 students onboarded, 193,875 impressions and reach, 2,186 likes, 789 comments and 334 saves**. I had earlier prepared the launch plan for PLP's community and served as a PLP ambassador at the University of Dar es Salaam (2023).

## Westerwelle Startup Haus Arusha, space reveal campaign (May to June 2022)

One of two influencers for the launch of the Arusha coworking space, on X and Instagram from 26 May to 2 June 2022. The relationship continued into a freelancing workshop proposal for 40 participants and a DigiTalent playbook in 2023.

## Mainstack brand representation (October 2022 to January 2023)

Invited by Mainstack's growth team to represent the brand to my audience for three months ("I must commend the incredible value you give to your community"). I sent a partnership concept note and a physical event estimate for 100 participants, and delivered a social media campaign in January 2023.

## What creator work taught me about running campaigns

Ask for the rate card first, report with the client's own analytics, and design the deliverable so it can be measured (a tracked link, a registration form, a promo code). The Education in Ireland numbers only exist because the campaign had a registration funnel. That is the discipline I now bring to the client side, in the [Miss Universe Tanzania campaign](miss_universe_tanzania.md) and the [influencer tiering playbook](../playbooks/influencer_campaign_tiers.md).

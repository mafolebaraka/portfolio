# Tanzania Spices Trademark · Social media audit for 13 producers

**Role:** Social media auditor and analyst, 2026
**Client context:** The Tanzania Spices Trademark, a TanTrade and TASPA initiative developed with the International Trade Centre under the EU MARKUP programme
**Deliverable:** A light scorecard across all 13 producers, then a data driven deep dive on five, delivered as Word documents

## The producers

Agri Promise Co. Ltd, Buyuni Bees, GFP Organics Ltd., Get Aroma, Get Group Co. Ltd, Gomzoe Investment Limited, Halisi Organic Farm, MIU Foods (Chimi Group), Makaru Agro Ltd., Niasili Sweet Investment, Savannah Grains Processing Ltd., Urithi Foods and Viridium Tanzania Limited.

## Method

I applied the audit framework I first built for Grey's social presence. Every producer was assessed across the same twelve sections: objectives, account list, profile overview, content analysis, engagement metrics, audience analysis, competitor analysis, what is working, what is not working, near term improvements, long term improvements and conclusion. Strength was expressed in plain labels (strong, middle tier, weak) so that producers with no marketing team could read it.

Round one produced the scorecard across all 13. Round two took five producers (Urithi Foods, Buyuni Bees, GFP Organics, Viridium Tanzania and Halisi Organic Farm) through fresh research and a full rewrite grounded in their actual account data.

The framework is published in this repository: [playbooks/social_media_audit.md](../playbooks/social_media_audit.md).

## Why it matters

Export oriented agricultural SMEs in Tanzania usually have a trademark, a stand at trade fairs and a dormant Facebook page. A comparable audit across all producers under one programme gives the programme office a way to see who needs help first and what kind.

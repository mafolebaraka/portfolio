# Platforms I founded and built

**Role:** Founder, product owner, marketer and (with collaborators) builder, 2026
**Entity:** Jukwaa Africa Limited and Remotika Africa

Marketers who can ship their own products understand distribution differently. These are the platforms I conceived, specified, positioned and launched in 2026. Each one started as a concept note and developer brief before it became code.

## Nijuze · [nijuze.co](https://nijuze.co)

A news site covering technology, social media, startups and artificial intelligence, with special editions on how to make money online. The social media category covers each platform individually, the AI category covers each major AI platform, and startups are split into Tanzania and global. English only.

Built on Next.js, hosted on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database, deploying from GitHub on push. Live at nijuze.co since August 2026 with 27 articles published in the first three weeks (22 August to 14 September), from "How mobile money became East Africa's operating system" to "TikTok monetisation for African creators" and "Jinsi ya kupata client online ukiwa Tanzania". Features a metered paywall (three free articles a month) with membership at TSh 5,000 monthly, 12,000 for three months, 21,000 for six and 36,000 a year (from TSh 3,000 a month), paid by mobile money (M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, AzamPesa) as one off payments with no auto renew, plus The Nijuze Brief newsletter and an advertise page. A newsroom at nijuze.co/desk with a rich text editor, inline images and video, editor and contributor roles and a review workflow, designed for 20 to 50 contributors. Aeonik is the official typeface.

## Mshahara · [mshahara.co](https://www.mshahara.co)

Salary transparency and jobs for East Africa. People share salaries anonymously and see others' (three free, then sign up, share your own and get verified by work email plus admin review), review current or past employers (anonymous, OTP protected, moderated before publishing), and create talent profiles. Companies post jobs, reach talent and get pay insights. Advertisers reach a professional audience through an advertiser signup and prepaid wallet.

Separate boards for local Tanzania jobs and foreign remote jobs in USD, first five free then a paid jobs pass via mobile money. Launched September 2026 (a product of Remotika Africa) with a free PAYE calculator for Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda, a CV Studio, a talent pool, advertiser placements from TZS 20,000, and points based AI add ons (explain my pay, compare two offers, check my payslip). Live on Cloudflare Workers, built with a developer partner through pull requests to a shared repository. Design system: "Midnight and Tanzanite" (deep navy ground, tanzanite blue violet accent, gold for money), Aeonik typeface. Features specified and shipped include a full admin dashboard, AI built employer profiles from companies' own websites (Qwen on Cloudflare Workers AI), SMS OTP via Briq, and specs for Insiders (opt in employee referral requests) and Internships for university students.

## Jukwaa · [jukwaa.co](https://jukwaa.co)

A premium digital products marketplace, live at jukwaa.co: courses, ebooks, templates, AI prompts, Notion and website templates, UI kits, presets, software, plugins, memberships and bundles. No physical products. Creators keep 90%, licences are written in plain language, the site runs in English and Kiswahili, and buyers can pay by card, M-Pesa, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay. A 53 page build with a creator studio (revenue, analytics, payouts) and a launch offer for creators who list before October 2026. WordPress with FluentCart and FluentAffiliate, with an affiliate programme (proposed tiers Starter 15%, Pro 20%, Partner 30%) with landing pages, a portal, leaderboard and marketing assets.

Navigation and filtering modelled on B&H Photo, benchmarked against Envato, Creative Market, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy and Adobe Marketplace. The design system was rebuilt in a bold creator marketplace direction: violet, lime and ink, with Archivo, Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono. Priorities in the brief: speed, WCAG accessibility, SEO schema and breadcrumbs, WordPress security practices and documentation of every significant change.

## mafolebaraka.com · my own platform on Wix

Relaunched in July 2026 as a Tanzanian digital skills platform under my own name: a bilingual blog (English and Swahili, five posts in the first quarter on translation work, freelancing and verifying platforms from Tanzania), a talent directory filterable by skill, nine self paced online programmes priced in TZS (from SEO copywriting at 25,000 to React for freelancers at 70,000), three digital products, events (a Freelance Portfolio Workshop in July and a Salary Transparency Community Talk in August 2026), four community groups, and Tanzanian payment methods (M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, Halotel, card). 442 sessions from 420 visitors in its first 60 days. Hosting is moving from Wix to Cloudflare for full ownership.

## What building taught me about marketing

Positioning has to be decided before the codebase. Every one of these started with three questions: who are the ICPs, what do they pay for, and which single payment rail do they already trust. In Tanzania that last answer is mobile money, every time.

# Rosalynn in Purple · Content strategy for an executive coaching brand

**Role:** Digital and Social Media Strategist and Community Manager, 2025 to 2026, through Jukwaa Africa Limited and PZG Impact Firm
**Client:** Rosalynn Mndolwa-Mworia, Managing Director of Mwananchi Communications Limited and The Citizen
**Channels:** Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp channel, Mailchimp newsletter, YouTube, The Citizen (opinion pieces), rosalynn.co.tz

## Context

Rosalynn in Purple is a personal brand built around women's leadership, ambition and personal growth, particularly for African women. I ran content strategy, community management and campaign production, working with the PZG team (video editing, design, WhatsApp channel management) and reporting to PZG's Chief People Officer.

## What I did

**Editorial programme.** Quarterly and monthly themes with a guardrail that all copy must empower the audience: Q1 "Leading from Your Why: Purpose as Your Strategic North Star", April "Future of Truth" and "AI and Leadership", May "Integration Over Balance". Deliverables included opinion pieces for The Citizen, LinkedIn articles, trending format Instagram captions, engagement polls and weekly open ended questions, with poll content kept distinct across Instagram, WhatsApp and newsletter formats.

**The Purple Talk video show.** Launch content (social captions, guest tease posts, a father reveal post, SVG artwork) and full episode briefs in a two part format: a Creative Directory (note to the team, creative guidance for the host, pre record checklist, B roll shot list, cold open) and a Run of Show (numbered segments, verbatim scripted questions, a "why this" rationale and clip targets per segment). Flagship episodes target 35 to 50 minutes and are cut into 6 to 10 vertical clips. Episodes developed include "Raising Human Beings: What Children Learn When We Are Not Looking" with educator Dr Edmund Mndolwa, and "Will AI Close the Leadership Gap for African Women, or Widen It?" with Irene Phoebe Kiwia of Adanian Labs and AICE Africa.

**RIPP 2.0 website relaunch.** A 35 day social media plan and a 13 page HTML site for rosalynn.co.tz, preceded by a five day pre launch campaign for the first version of the site.

**The Purple Circle newsletter.** Subscriber re engagement with segmented re introduction emails for Rising Woman event attendees and one to one contacts, a Mailchimp versus Flodesk platform comparison, and a granular January to March performance report for internal use followed by a client facing version.

**Brand voice.** Every piece written in Rosalynn's verified voice: British spelling, no em dashes, a "Rosalynn, in Purple" sign off, and the hashtag set #RosalynnInPurple #WomenWhoLead #AfricanLeadership.

## Results

* A show format and brief template now used for every episode of The Purple Talk
* A relaunched website with a coordinated 35 day social campaign
* A measurement baseline for the newsletter and Instagram, with UTM tracking and downloadable lead magnets identified as the next gaps to close
* Testimonial and social proof content sourced only from real follower comments and DMs

## What I learned

Surface problems alongside solutions. My reports to the client team were transparent about limitations, paired with concrete recommendations and framed as opportunities. Get a trend's mechanics right before writing to it. And never fabricate social proof; source it.

# Gladness Kifaluka · Brand and website for an actor and director

**Role:** Brand strategist and web builder, 2026
**Client:** Gladness Kifaluka, Tanzanian actor and popular director
**Scope:** Personal brand, website and an attached shop for her perfume line

## Brief

Build a brand home for a screen personality that serves three audiences at once: fans, the industry (producers, casting, press) and buyers of her first product line, perfume.

## Decisions

* **Visual direction:** cinematic luxury. Charcoal, gold and editorial photography.
* **Sections:** filmography, about and bio, press and media kit, booking and contact, shop.
* **Shop flow:** WhatsApp plus M-Pesa ordering rather than a card gateway, because that is how her audience already buys in Tanzania.
* **Language:** English only for the first version.

## Why the shop flow matters

Card gateways add friction and cost for a single product line sold to a local audience. A WhatsApp conversation ending in an M-Pesa payment converts better in this market and doubles as a customer relationship channel.

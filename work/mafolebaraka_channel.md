# @mafolebaraka · Bilingual video first channel

**Role:** Creator, strategist, host, 2021 to present
**Platforms:** YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Telegram, WhatsApp Channel, Substack, plus a podcast on Spotify and Apple Podcasts
**Languages:** Swahili and English

This file covers how the channel runs. The story of how it was built, and the strategy behind it, is in [personal_brand.md](personal_brand.md).

## Context

@mafolebaraka is my own channel on remote work, freelancing and the creator economy, aimed first at young Tanzanians and Kenyans and then at a global English speaking audience. The Swahili content uses authentic street Bongo flavour rather than a formal register. The English content positions me as an operator thinker on digital jobs, startups and the creator economy.

## How it runs

**Monthly themes.** Each month is anchored on one theme (May 2026 was freelancing) with a consistent daily rhythm: a morning hook, a midday tactical post, an evening community or engagement post. Content is published daily across Substack, the WhatsApp Channel, the Telegram Channel, the Instagram Broadcast Channel and TikTok.

**Modular long form.** Long pieces are structured for repurposing into carousels, threads and newsletter intros.

**Video.** Swahili explainers on online jobs, translation and transcription work, AI training gigs, websites that pay, and freelancing cohorts. Sponsored video pricing includes a three month promotion and usage window per video.

**Publication.** Moments with Mafolebaraka, an English language publication on LinkedIn and Substack with three editorial pillars: digital jobs, startups, creator economy.

**Content pipeline.** Substack and Medium for adapted essays, Canva and CapCut for production, Audacity for audio, Ahrefs and Flodesk for analytics.

## Point of view

I argue that every company is a media company, and that the interesting question is why media companies still want to remain media companies. My UNESCO talking points covered algorithmic disadvantage for Swahili content, NLP tool gaps, and monetisation platform exclusions for East African creators.

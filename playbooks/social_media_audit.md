# Social media audit framework

The twelve section format I use for brand audits. First built for Grey Finance's social presence, reused for the 13 producers under the Tanzania Spices Trademark, and for my own digital brand audit.

## Structure

1. **Objectives.** What the brand is trying to achieve on social right now, in one or two sentences. If the brand does not know, say so and propose objectives.
2. **Account list.** Every live handle, with URL, follower count, date of last post and who manages it.
3. **Profile overview.** Bio, link in bio, profile image, highlights, pinned content, contact details. Is the profile doing its job in five seconds?
4. **Content analysis.** Post frequency, format mix (video, static, carousel, stories, live), themes, language, tone, production quality, sample of the last 30 posts.
5. **Engagement metrics.** Engagement rate per platform, reach where available, best and worst performing posts and why.
6. **Audience analysis.** Who follows, where they are, what they respond to, gaps between the actual audience and the intended customer.
7. **Competitor analysis.** Three to five comparable brands on the same platforms, with the same metrics side by side.
8. **What is working.** Specific, evidence backed.
9. **What is not working.** Specific, evidence backed, no hedging.
10. **Near term improvements.** Changes possible in the next 30 days with the current team and budget.
11. **Long term improvements.** Changes that need new resources, content formats or positioning.
12. **Conclusion.** One overall rating and the three things to do first.

## Rating scale

Use plain labels: **strong**, **middle tier**, **weak**. Business owners without a marketing team can act on those. They cannot act on a 6.4 out of 10.

## Rules

* Pull real numbers from the accounts. Screenshots beat descriptions.
* Compare to direct competitors, not global benchmarks, unless the brand sells internationally.
* Every "not working" point must have a matching improvement in section 10 or 11.
* Keep the scorecard version to one page per brand when auditing many brands under one programme; keep the deep dive for the brands that will act on it.
